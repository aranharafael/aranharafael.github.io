Welcome! I'm a professor at [Rio de Janeiro State University](https://www.uerj.br/), in the [Institute of Physics](https://www.fis.uerj.br/), a member of the [Department of Theoretical Physics](https://www.fis.uerj.br/index.php/departamento-de-fisica-teorica/) and active in the Gravitation and Cosmology group. 

I received my bachelor’s degree in Physics from the same university in 2006, and my PhD in Physical Sciences from the [Brazilian Center for Physics Research](https://www.gov.br/cbpf/pt-br) in 2011. I then spent two years as a postdoctoral fellow at the [Center for Relativistic Astrophysics](https://cra.gatech.edu/), part of the [School of Physics](https://physics.gatech.edu/) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), in Atlanta, USA. 

My work lies at the interface between theoretical physics, mathematics and computation, with the common goal of making the tools used in the study of gravitation more rigorous and reliable. In [Numerical Relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_relativity), I focus on the mathematical foundations of simulations, such as the well-posedness of formulations, the role of constraints and gauge choices, and the behavior of numerical errors. I am also interested in mathematical methods of gravitation, including the representation of geometric objects and black hole solutions of Einstein’s equations, as well as in numerical analysis and spectral methods in the light of functional analysis. Alongside research, I am deeply committed to teaching theoretical physics with mathematical rigor, through courses, textbooks and the training of students from the early stages of their undergraduate studies, with the aim of strengthening this line of research in Brazil.
